# Useful Resources for Exoplanet High Contrast Imaging

## System Properties
* **Exoplanet Archive**: NASA Exoplanet Archive, repository of discovered exoplanets and their bulk parameters. [Link](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/)
* **Exoplanet EU**: European Exoplanet Archive. [Link](https://exoplanet.eu/home/)
* **ExoMAST**: NASA Exoplanet Archive's MAST interface, helps with searching and downloading exoplanet data from MAST. [Link](https://exo.mast.stsci.edu/)
* **UltracoolSheet**: Database of ultracool dwarfs and directly imaged exoplanets, with photometry, spectra, and astrometry. [Link](https://zenodo.org/records/15802304)
* **Exopost**: A browser for planet & companion posteriors from Gaia + Hipparcos astrometry. [Link](https://exopo.st/)

## Observing
* **SVO Filter Profile Service**: Database of filter transmission curves for many instruments. [Link](https://svo2.cab.inta-csic.es/theory/fps/)
* **TrExoLiSTS**: A list of JWST transiting exoplanet observations [Link](https://www.stsci.edu/~nnikolov/TrExoLiSTS/JWST/trexolists.html)
* **MAST**: Mikulski Archive for Space Telescopes, repository of HST, JWST, and other space telescope data. [Link](https://mast.stsci.edu/portal/Mashup/Clients/Mast/Portal.html)

## Models
* **Species Model List**: List of publicly available atmospheric and evolutionary model grids used by the species repository. [Link](https://species.readthedocs.io/en/latest/data.html)  
* **Sonora Flame Skimmer**: Not yet in species, for cool substellar objects. [Link](https://zenodo.org/records/20030439)
* **Stellar Color-Temperature Sequence**: Table of stellar effective temperatures and colors for main sequence stars, from Pecaut & Mamajek (2013). [Link](https://www.pas.rochester.edu/~emamajek/EEM_dwarf_UBVIJHK_colors_Teff.txt)

## Figures
